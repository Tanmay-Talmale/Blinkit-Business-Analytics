# 💡 Business Insights

## 1. Revenue Growth

### Business Question

Is Blinkit's revenue growing consistently over time?

### Analysis

Revenue and order volume are analyzed at yearly and monthly levels using SQL.

### Business Impact

This helps management identify growth periods and potential seasonality.

---

## 2. Store Performance

### Business Question

Which stores generate the highest revenue?

### Analysis

Stores are ranked by revenue and order volume.

### Business Impact

High-performing stores can be prioritized for capacity planning and operational investment.

---

## 3. Customer Retention

### Business Question

Are customers returning to place additional orders?

### Analysis

First-order, repeat-order and reorder behaviour are analyzed.

### Business Impact

This helps evaluate customer retention and identify opportunities for improving repeat purchases.

---

## 4. Operational Risk

### Business Question

Which stores require operational attention?

### Analysis

Stores are evaluated using:

- Order volume
- Failure rate
- Delivery time
- Customer rating

### Business Impact

Management can prioritize operational interventions at stores with multiple performance issues.

---

## 5. Promotion Effectiveness

### Business Question

Are promotions generating meaningful business value?

### Analysis

Promoted and non-promoted orders are compared using:

- AOV
- Order quantity
- Revenue
- Discount

### Business Impact

This helps optimize promotional spending.
