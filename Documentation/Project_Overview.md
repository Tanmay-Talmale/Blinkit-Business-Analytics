# Blinkit Business Analytics — Project Overview

## Role

Data Analyst & Business Analyst

## Objective

Analyze Blinkit's transactional order data to identify revenue opportunities, customer behaviour patterns, operational issues and business risks.

## Business Areas

### 1. Revenue

- Revenue growth
- AOV
- Monthly trends
- Store revenue
- City revenue

### 2. Customers

- Customer tiers
- Customer lifetime value
- Repeat behaviour
- Reorder rate
- Cohort retention
- Churn risk

### 3. Operations

- Delivery time
- Order failures
- Cancellation/return reasons
- Store efficiency
- Customer ratings

### 4. Promotions

- Promo usage
- Promo revenue
- Discount impact
- Promo contribution
